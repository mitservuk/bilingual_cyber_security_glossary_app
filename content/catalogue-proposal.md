# Proposed catalogue: certifications and frameworks

A proposal to review, trim and add to. It lists what the app's Certifications
and Frameworks categories could cover. Nothing here is built yet beyond CCSP.

Being listed does not mean getting its own set of terms straight away. Most
vocabulary is shared, so each term is written once and tagged to every exam
and framework it belongs to. The order in which these are filled in is a
separate decision.

Exam names and codes change often. They are given as generally known and need
confirming against each issuer before anything is published.

## Questions this raises

- Which of these matter to you, and which are missing?
- FIPS 140-3 and the US frameworks originate in the United States but are used
  worldwide. The filter treats them by origin; say if you would rather tag by
  where they apply.
- Some items are both an exam and a framework (ITIL, TOGAF, ISO/IEC 27001).
  They are listed in both places here.
